About ME

WHo AM i

Farid Fatkhur Rozi

Born in Sidoarjo on March 7, 2004, I am a person who has grown with a passion for learning and a wealth of life experiences. My childhood began in Kediri, where I spent joyful days with my grandmother during kindergarten. When I entered elementary school, life’s journey brought me back to Sidoarjo, which became my home until I graduated from middle school.

During middle school, I had the valuable opportunity to study at the Al-Hidayah Islamic Boarding School in Ketegan, Sidoarjo. There, I learned a lot about discipline, religious knowledge, and the importance of brotherhood.

My desire for continuous growth led me to continue my education at a vocational high school in Kediri, at the Hidayatus Sholihin Islamic Boarding School in Turus, Gurah. However, after one year, I felt a stronger call to return to Sidoarjo, and I resumed my studies at SMK TPI Gedangan.

Initially, I chose the Computer Network Engineering major, but my passion for technology and innovation drove me to switch to Software Engineering. The year 2024 marked an important moment in my life—I proudly graduated from SMK TPI Gedangan, ready to face the tech world with the knowledge I have gained.

I am someone who believes that every step in life, whether big or small, shapes us into better individuals. And I always strive to be the best version of myself, every day.

My Journey from Kediri to Sidoarjo: Chasing Dreams in the World of IT

In 2021, I was just an ordinary student at SMK Hidayatus Sholihin in Kediri, majoring in Computer and Network Engineering (TKJ). At that time, the world of IT felt like a vast ocean — I only knew its surface, but I was curious about what lay beneath. But life sometimes throws unexpected changes at you. In my second year, I had to move to Sidoarjo. Was it tough? Absolutely. I had to leave my friends behind and adapt to a new environment at SMK TPI Gedangan. There, I chose Software Engineering (RPL) as my major — it felt like a fresh start, more focused, and closer to my true passion. In that same year, I got the chance to intern at CV. Development RMDOO, an IT company. That’s when everything started to feel real. I wasn’t just learning how to code or debug, but I was also experiencing the real work environment: deadlines, teamwork, clients, and even learning how to face failure. Every day felt like opening a mystery box — sometimes stressful, sometimes rewarding, but always a learning experience. After graduation, I didn’t stop. I continued my studies, majoring in Informatics. It’s not easy, but I always remind myself: this isn’t just about getting a degree or good grades; it’s about the dream. Someday, I want to become someone who not only works in IT but also makes an impact. Maybe build a startup, maybe develop an app that makes people’s lives easier — who knows?
